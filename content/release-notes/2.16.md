This update brings new features and improvements:

- Voice actor and staff roles screens now have sorting and filtering options! Filter to show items only on your list, and sort media by airing date, title, and more
- Discover has even more ways to find media: newly-added anime and manga, plus trending and highest rated anime of all time.
- Following/followers lists are now sorted alphabetically
- Updated appearance of active filter buttons in navigation bar
- Updated design of sort menus on Dashboard and List
- Fixed an invalid option appearing in the manga Dashboard sort menu
- The List screen uses a new search tool under the hood, which should lead to slightly better results and faster searches for large lists
